Business intelligence · ITB final project · 2026

Retail inventory intelligence

An integrated BI system for inventory and demand planning at an automotive-parts retailer.

Role

Author · ITB thesis

Tools & methods

Metabase · Data warehousing · Demand forecasting

Thesis proposal ↗

Original thesis proposal

Problem

Toko XYZ relied on fragmented records and intuition for stock decisions.

My contribution

I unified sales and product-search records in a warehouse that refreshes daily. I evaluated forecasts by product, then built five dashboards and a Telegram bot so the retailer could use those forecasts when planning stock orders.

Approach

  1. Centralize

    Unified sales and product-search data in a warehouse with daily automated updates.

  2. Model demand

    Classified demand with Syntetos–Boylan and evaluated 16 time-series model variants per product.

  3. Support decisions

    Delivered five interactive dashboards and a Telegram bot for stock and reordering recommendations.

Delivered

A connected inventory-analysis system, from warehouse updates to demand forecasts and stock recommendations.

ITB Thesis · Author · Data & Algorithms

Retail Inventory Intelligence

An integrated BI system for inventory and demand planning at an automotive-parts retailer.

Background

My ITB thesis supported an Indonesian small business (UMKM) that made stock decisions manually. I explored how sales and product-search data could make those decisions more informed.

Key Decisions

Connect Records to Stock Decisions

Combine sales and product-search records in a daily-updated warehouse, then make forecasts available through dashboards and a stock-information bot.

Results

The business measured and reported a reduction in the entire ordering process from seven working days to a maximum of three hours. The system brought trend reporting and stock planning into one workflow.

Scope & Limitations: The time saving was measured and reported by the business. Reductions in overstock or stockouts were not separately measured. Public screens use fictional items and figures to protect the retailer’s data.

Contribution

I combined the records in a daily-updated warehouse, evaluated demand forecasts and built five dashboards plus a Telegram stock bot.

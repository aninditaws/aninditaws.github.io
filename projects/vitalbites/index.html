VitalBites

A gamified healthy-eating prototype with photo, voice and manual food logging.

Read project report ↗: Read project report (opens in a new tab)

Background

A four-person HCI project exploring gamified healthy eating, with photo, voice and manual food logging.

My contribution

I designed seven food-input screens, from the calorie overview through photo capture/results, manual entry/search and voice input. I also wrote the usability-test script.

Result

All five test participants completed the camera-input task in the prototype. The work demonstrates an interaction concept, rather than production AI recognition.

What testing taught us

Testing exposed navigation and button-size issues that informed the team’s revisions.

  • Figma
  • User research
  • Prototyping
  • Usability testing
  • SUS
  • Impact–effort prioritization

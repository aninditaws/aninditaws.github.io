Background

A team optimization experiment comparing approaches to arranging a 5 × 5 × 5 diagonal magic cube.

My contribution

I implemented the genetic algorithm. Our team compared it with hill climbing and simulated annealing using deviations from the target sum as the objective.

Result

An algorithm implementation with objective-history plots. The displayed plot documents a single search run.

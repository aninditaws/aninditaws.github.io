Restu Mande Inventory Model

An inventory decision model exploring when to order ingredients and how much to buy.

Background

An inventory-modeling assignment asking when to order ingredients and how much to buy while balancing ordering and holding costs.

Key Decisions

Model Inventory Constraints Explicitly

Use an integer linear program with inventory and capacity constraints, under constant-demand and fixed-lead-time assumptions.

Results

An ordering model with recommendations under constant demand and fixed lead times. Its use depends on those assumptions holding.

Contribution

Our team formulated an integer linear program and solved it in Excel under inventory and capacity constraints.
